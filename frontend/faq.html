Frequently Asked Questions

What is VelvetWhisp?

VelvetWhisp is an online website and curated database for fans of cinema's most iconic creatures. We provide detailed information, lore, and behind-the-scenes trivia for movies and series centered around vampires, werewolves, and other supernatural beings.

What content can I expect?

You can expect a deep dive into the worlds of your favorite films and shows. Our content includes detailed plot summaries, character biographies, and unique lore sections that explore the specific rules of each universe—from the sparkling vampires of 'Twilight' to the ancient bloodlines of 'The Originals' and the daywalking abilities of 'Blade'. We also provide links to trailers and information on where to watch.
