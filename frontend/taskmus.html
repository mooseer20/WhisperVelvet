Louvre Museum (Paris)

The Louvre is one of the world's largest and most famous museums, home to iconic masterpieces such as the Mona Lisa and the Venus de Milo. Originally a fortress built in the 12th century, it now houses over 380,000 objects.

British Museum (London)

Known for the Rosetta Stone and the Elgin Marbles, the British Museum displays world history, culture, and human evolution from ancient Egypt to modern civilizations.

Metropolitan Museum of Art (New York)

The MET is one of the world’s largest art museums, showcasing over 2 million works from 5,000 years of human creativity, spanning Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas.
