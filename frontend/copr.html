Our Use of Cookies

VelvetWhisp uses a minimal number of cookies to ensure the basic functionality of the website. These are known as "strictly necessary" cookies and do not store any personally identifiable information.

What are Cookies?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device that helps a website remember information about your visit. This can make your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

Managing Cookies

As we only use essential cookies, there are no preferences to manage on this site. You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish by adjusting your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of this and many other websites that you visit.
